# Civic-Chain × Broadband Telecom: A Partnership Proposal

**Created:** 2026-10-14. **Prepared for:** Broadband Telecom Services. **From:** Nick Hernandez, Civic-Chain.

What this is. A proposal to turn the municipal and grant relationships Broadband Telecom already owns into a recurring revenue stream, by co-selling Civic-Chain's public-money accountability platform into the towns and agencies you already serve.

The offer. **10% of the net profit Civic-Chain earns on every deal you source, for the full life of the contract.** You open the door and co-sell. We build, deliver, and support. The share is recurring, and it grows as each town climbs to the next product.

---

## 1. Why Civic-Chain, and why now

Every town you serve moves public money, contracts, grants, vendor payments, and almost none of it can be independently verified. Residents are handed portals full of numbers no one can audit. Auditors reconcile by hand, months later. And federal programs now demand proof that every grant dollar was spent as promised.

- **The problem.** Towns move billions through contracts and grant programs on open-data portals that publish figures no one can verify and spreadsheets no one can audit. When a grant is questioned or a payment goes wrong, there is no single record that proves what actually happened.
- **The product.** Civic-Chain is one verifiable record per public contract: a public, tamper-evident ledger residents and auditors can query in plain English, spend cards that only pay a vendor after work is confirmed, and AI that reconciles every figure to the books or refuses to answer.
- **The timing.** Federal programs, BEAD broadband, IIJA, ARPA, and improper-payment laws now require towns to prove accountability for every public dollar. The buyers you already serve are being told to do exactly what our platform does.

## 2. The product your towns would be buying

Three products on one engine, sold as a ladder: a town starts with the cheap, easy wedge and expands up over time. Because the engine is schema-agnostic, every new product is a new allow-list, not new plumbing.

| Stage | Product | What it does | What it replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Land, the wedge** | **TLaaS, the Transparency Layer** | A public, tamper-evident record of every public dollar, queryable in plain English, with unlimited users and every figure checked against the books. | Legacy open-data portals (Socrata, OpenGov) at roughly a tenth of the cost |
| **Expand, the prize** | **Vendor Management + spend cards** | Vendor payments on locked cards, released only after a milestone is confirmed, with duplicate and over-contract detection. | Enterprise contract software and the 2.25–2.75% incumbent payment fees we undercut |
| **Attach, the upsell** | **Verified Intelligence** | Ask about the books in plain English; every figure is reconciled to the data or the system refuses to answer, re-runnable to the identical result. | The annual audit and compliance software |

The subscription starts small and recurring; the payment product adds a small usage fee once the town runs spend through our rails. This is recurring, high-margin software and payments, not one-time services, which is exactly why a profit share on it is worth having.

## 5. The partnership: 10% of net profit on every deal you source

The structure is deliberately simple. You do what you are already good at, opening doors in the public sector, and you share in the profit of everything that follows.

- **What you bring.** Warm introductions and co-selling into the municipal and grant relationships you already have. *No build. No support. No capital.*
- **What you earn.** 10% of the net profit Civic-Chain earns on every contract you originate, for the full life of that contract, including renewals and every product the town adds up the ladder. *Recurring, not a one-time referral fee.*
- **How it's paid.** Attribution is agreed in writing when a deal is registered to you; the share is paid on recognized, collected revenue, on a regular schedule. *Transparent and auditable, the same standard our product holds everyone to.*

### Why the 10% is real money

- Civic-Chain is a **high-margin, recurring business**: subscription software plus a small fee on the payments a town runs through our rails. The software margins are high and the revenue renews every year.
- **"Net profit on a deal you source"** means the revenue recognized from that account minus the direct costs to deliver and run it, hosting, payment-rail, and support. It is not a slice of gross revenue and not a one-time bounty; it is a standing share of the profit that account produces for as long as it stays with us.
- Because governments rarely switch vendors and towns **climb from the wedge to the payment product over time**, the profit on each relationship, and your share of it, grows year over year without you selling again.
- Exact definitions, attribution windows, and payment terms are set in a short written partner agreement. This page is the offer, not the contract.
